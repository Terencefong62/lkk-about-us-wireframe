A Century of Heritage

Lee Kum Kee was founded in 1888 by Mr. Lee Kum Sheung in Nanshui, Zhuhai, Guangdong Province of China, where our legendary journey began with a single wok of oyster sauce.

Since then, the flavours of Lee Kum Kee have been part of treasured family moments around the table for generations. Today, we’ve grown into a global brand well-known as a mark of quality and trust.

Guided by the values of “Si Li Ji Ren”, “Pragmatism”, “Constant Entrepreneurship” and “100-1=0”, and further strengthened by our principles of “Autopilot Leadership” and “Leading Change”, Lee Kum Kee has straddled three centuries. From our headquarters in Hong Kong, China, we connect with the world, supported by production bases in Xinhui, Huangpu and Jining, China; Los Angeles and Newnan in the USA; and Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

With a mission of “Promoting Chinese Culinary Culture Worldwide”, Lee Kum Kee offers over 300 products enjoyed by consumers in more than 100 countries and regions around the globe. Rooted in rich heritage and committed to quality, excellence and sustainability, we continue to innovate by prioritising the needs of consumers as we bring authentic flavours to the world.

03 UNESCO

Championing Culinary
Heritage

Culinary culture is central to Lee Kum Kee’s purpose. Through our partnership with UNESCO, we are extending that commitment to the safeguarding and transmission of culinary heritage around the world.

Together, we have launched the Forever Flavors Project to collect food-related stories from across the world and create an archive of flavour memories. The initiative uses flavour as a bridge between cultures and generations, encouraging dialogue, deepening emotional connections and helping culinary knowledge and traditions endure.

This partnership reflects our long-term commitment to championing culinary culture. Grounded in shared values and respect for heritage, it demonstrates how public and private organisations can work together to support cultural safeguarding and the continued transmission of culinary heritage.

of Culinary Ideas

Lee Kum Kee is The World’s 50 Best Restaurants’ first-ever exclusive Official Sauce & Condiment Partner. The partnership places our sauce and culinary expertise in conversation with chefs who are shaping dining around the world. Through chef engagement, industry moments and shared exploration, we gain closer insight into how professional kitchens work, while giving chefs a trusted platform from which to test ideas, reinterpret flavours and create for the next generation of diners.

06 Our Commitments

How Responsibility
Becomes Practice

Our commitments are not separate from how we operate. They shape the standards we set, the decisions we make and the way we consider people and the environment across our value chain. Two ideas provide the clearest expression of this approach: 100-1=0, our uncompromising quality philosophy, and a sustainability agenda focused on product integrity, environmental protection, employees and communities.

BACKGROUND IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
QUALITY CONTROL

100-1=0

100-1=0 means that one failure can negate every success that came before it. The philosophy therefore asks every function—not only quality teams—to protect the integrity of products and services. It informs how we assess ingredients and suppliers, control production, verify finished products, respond to issues and keep improving the systems behind every product.
